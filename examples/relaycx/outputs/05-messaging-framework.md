## Positioning statement

**For** Mid-market US banks, credit unions and consumer lenders, and outpatient provider groups and multisite specialty practices **who** need routine customer and patient requests completed without adding staff at the same rate or taking on new regulatory risk, **RelayCX** is an agentic AI customer experience platform for email, SMS and web chat **that** finishes administrative tasks, such as collecting loan documents or rescheduling appointments, and verifies the result before confirming it. **Unlike** chatbots that only answer questions, and AI agent platforms built for large enterprises or a single industry, **we** pair verified completion with industry-specific action boundaries, identity checks and contextual handoffs to staff [ASSUMPTION] (fictional capabilities, differentiation to be validated).

## Elevator pitch

Routine service requests get finished across email, SMS and web chat, inside the boundaries you set, and everything else reaches your staff with full context. That is what RelayCX does.
