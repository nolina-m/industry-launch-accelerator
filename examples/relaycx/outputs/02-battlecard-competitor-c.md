## Where we lose

- the CRM vendor-centric shops whose IT team wants one platform [ASSUMPTION].
- Buyers who can bundle Competitor C into an existing enterprise agreement [ASSUMPTION].

## Landmine questions to plant in discovery

- Which systems outside the CRM vendor will the agent need to act in, and how is that integration built and maintained?
- What the CRM vendor editions and data services are required for the use cases you want?

## Traps to avoid

- Bundled pricing that makes a point solution look expensive. Compare cost per completed task, including integration work.
- Platform consolidation arguments. Ask who owns building and maintaining the workflows.

- **Reframe:** The question is whether the work is in the CRM vendor or in your core, loan servicing or scheduling systems.
- **Evidence:** Proposed integrations include the CRM vendor itself [ASSUMPTION] (not yet built).
