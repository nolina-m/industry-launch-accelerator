## Where we win

- Shared context across email, SMS and web chat for patients who start in one channel and finish in another [ASSUMPTION].

## Where we lose

- Practices that want a healthcare-only vendor with deep EHR integrations and references [ASSUMPTION].

## Landmine questions to plant in discovery

- Can a patient start a reschedule in web chat and confirm it by SMS without starting over?
- Which EHR and practice management systems are integrated today, and at what depth?

## Traps to avoid

- EHR integration depth as the only decision criterion. RelayCX cannot claim EHR integrations yet, so position a pilot on a contained workflow [ASSUMPTION].

## Top objections when they're in the deal

### "Competitor E already integrates with our EHR."

- **Underlying concern:** Integration risk.
- **Usually from:** VP Patient Access, IT
- **Acknowledge:** EHR integration is essential for scheduling [VERIFY].
- **Reframe:** Integration is necessary; the question is what the agent does with it across channels.
- **Evidence:** [VERIFY] RelayCX EHR integrations are proposed, not built.
- **Ask back:** Which EHR and scheduling actions would a pilot need?
- **Proof:** [PROOF NEEDED] Working EHR scheduling integration.

### "Competitor E already fills open slots automatically."

- **Underlying concern:** Overlap with an existing tool.
- **Usually from:** Scheduling leader
- **Acknowledge:** Automated rebooking is valuable [VERIFY].
- **Reframe:** Fill rate is one outcome; patients also need inbound requests completed across channels.
- **Evidence:** [PROOF NEEDED] Inbound completion data.
- **Ask back:** What share of reschedule requests come in by message rather than phone?
- **Proof:** [PROOF NEEDED] Channel mix from a pilot.
