## Resolved direction

Resolved direction (PMM accepted all strategy-check points and additions):

- Industries: (1) Financial services: US mid-market regional banks, credit unions and non-bank consumer lenders, with consumer lenders treated as their own sub-segment because the FTC Safeguards Rule applies to them. (2) Healthcare: US mid-market outpatient provider groups and multisite specialty practices; life sciences is out of scope.
- What each industry prioritizes: financial services buys controlled execution and traceability that risk and compliance teams can examine. Healthcare buys patient access and less administrative workload, but privacy, security and AI disclosure decide whether a deal closes.
- Buyers: financial services economic buyers are the VP Customer Service or COO, championed by contact center and operations directors. Healthcare economic buyers are the practice's senior operations leader (practice administrator, COO or VP Patient Access, depending on the group), championed by scheduling and patient experience leaders. IT, security, privacy and compliance are gatekeepers in both industries who can stop a deal, and they get their own materials from day one.
- Competition: the closest threats are industry specialists (Competitor D in banking, Competitor E in healthcare) and Competitor C where the CRM vendor is installed; Competitor A, Competitor B, the incumbent platform, building internally and the status quo are also covered. Never assume feature superiority.
- Integrations: treat integration as the main competitive gap, not just a research question. Plan each hero workflow around systems with usable APIs, and be open that no connector is built yet.
- Story: lead with verified completion of an administrative task, inside clear action boundaries and with contextual human handoffs. "From customer request to completed task" remains the working line, but the differentiator is verified completion with visible boundaries; email, SMS and web chat continuity is supporting proof, not the headline.
- Heroes: healthcare leads with appointment rescheduling, expecting head-on competition from Competitor E. Financial services uses loan document collection and receipt confirmation as the working hero, to be validated in discovery, with a backup hero drawn from service requests that drive repeat contacts.
- Additions accepted: (SA-1) configurable AI disclosure by state and channel is a launch requirement; (SA-2) ship a security and due diligence pack with the launch; (SA-3) SMS consent and opt-out handling is an explicit capability; (SA-4) mergers are a buying trigger in financial services; (SA-5) build a proof plan before launch with design partners, pilot baselines and success thresholds.
- Claims we never make: no invented customers, testimonials, ROI or performance results; no guaranteed compliance, zero-error AI, or unverified certifications and integrations; no claim that the agent approves loans, gives investment advice, diagnoses, recommends treatment or replaces staff. Answer accuracy concerns (including competitors' hallucination guarantees) with controls and verification, not a guarantee. Fictional demos stay labeled as illustrations, and every asset carries the fictional-product label.
