### WS-1: The problem

Your chatbot answers questions. Then the member emails, texts and calls about the same request, and your team starts over each time. Loan documents get chased by hand, and nobody can see which step stalled.

### WS-2: Why now

Most credit unions and about half of banks already use generative AI. Regulators expect any AI channel to give customers a path to a person, and fraudsters are using deepfakes to get past ID checks. The next step is finishing requests safely, not adding another bot.

### WS-3: How it works

Understand the request, then verify identity using your methods. Retrieve approved information and records, collect what's missing and take permitted actions. Finally, confirm the result, or hand off to your team with context.

### WS-4: Value by role

Operations leaders: serve members like a national bank, without a national bank's AI budget. Contact center leaders: requests arrive done, or with every step so far. Risk and compliance: see exactly what the agent did and when a person stepped in. IT: add workflows through the APIs you already support.

### WS-5: Controls

You decide what the agent may do. Sensitive actions require verification, fee adjustments and other high-risk changes require staff approval, SMS consent is respected, and every access and action is logged [ASSUMPTION] (fictional capability). RelayCX never makes lending decisions or gives financial advice.

### WS-6: FAQ

Will examiners accept this? It's built for examinable controls and a clear path to a person; your compliance team decides. Does it replace staff? No; it takes routine work so your team can focus on members who need them. What about our core? Start with a workflow on a system that already has an API.
