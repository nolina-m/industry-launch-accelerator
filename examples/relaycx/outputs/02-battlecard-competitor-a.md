## Where we win

- Mid-market focus with packaged banking, lending and patient access workflows, rather than a custom build with a large-enterprise vendor [ASSUMPTION].
- Completion is verified before reporting success, and blocked or failed actions are visible to staff [ASSUMPTION].

## Where we lose

- Buyers who want voice in the first release; RelayCX excludes voice [ASSUMPTION].
- Buyers who weigh brand, scale and reference logos above fit [ASSUMPTION].

## Landmine questions to plant in discovery

- How will you define a billable resolution, and who decides whether a conversation was resolved?
- Which banks, credit unions or outpatient groups of our size are live, and on which workflows?
- When the agent changes a record, how does it confirm the change actually happened before telling the customer?

## Traps to avoid

- Leading with voice demos and brand logos to make channel breadth the decision criterion. Stay on the task the customer needs finished and how it is verified.
- Framing outcome-based pricing as zero risk. Ask how resolution is measured and audited.
