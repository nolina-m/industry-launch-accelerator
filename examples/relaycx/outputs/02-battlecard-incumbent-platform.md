## Landmine questions to plant in discovery

- What share of chatbot conversations today end with the task actually done, versus a handoff or a repeat contact?
- Can your current bot take an action in a core or scheduling system, or does it only answer?

## Traps to avoid

- 'Our vendor's roadmap includes this.' Ask for dates and what is generally available today.

## Top objections when they're in the deal

### "Our current vendor is adding AI agents."

- **Underlying concern:** Avoiding a new vendor.
- **Usually from:** Contact center director, IT
- **Acknowledge:** Contact center vendors are adding agentic features [S49].
- **Reframe:** The test is whether the feature completes your specific workflows, with your controls, this year.
- **Evidence:** [VERIFY] the incumbent's generally available capabilities.
- **Ask back:** Which workflow would you want finished end to end first, and can your vendor show it live?
- **Proof:** [PROOF NEEDED] Side-by-side pilot results.

### "We already have a chatbot."

- **Underlying concern:** Sunk cost.
- **Usually from:** VP Customer Service
- **Acknowledge:** Many institutions have a chatbot today.
- **Reframe:** Answering questions and completing tasks are different jobs.
- **Evidence:** Regulators note chatbots lose effectiveness on complex problems [S9].
- **Ask back:** How many chatbot conversations end in a call or a second contact?
- **Proof:** [PROOF NEEDED] Repeat-contact baseline.
