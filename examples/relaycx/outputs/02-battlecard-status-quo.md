## Overview

## Top objections when they're in the deal

### "Our current process works well enough."

- **Underlying concern:** Change risk.
- **Usually from:** COO
- **Acknowledge:** If service levels are fine, change has to earn its place.
- **Reframe:** The question is the cost of keeping it that way as volume and staffing shift.
- **Evidence:** Patient access roles remain turnover hotspots [S23].
- **Ask back:** What would it take to measure repeat contacts and handoffs for a month?
