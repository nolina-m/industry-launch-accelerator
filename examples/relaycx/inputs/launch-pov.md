# Launch POV

Proposed launch direction for a fictional toolkit test. Assumptions are hypotheses for research, not validated market facts.

## Target industries

- Financial services: US regional banks and credit unions, mid-market.
- Healthcare and life sciences: US outpatient provider groups and multisite specialty practices, mid-market.

## Why these industries

[ASSUMPTION] Both have recurring administrative requests that require information retrieval, coordination, and system updates. We believe financial services will prioritize controlled execution and traceability, while healthcare will prioritize patient access and reduced administrative workload. Research should test these hypotheses and whether integrations make the proposed use cases practical.

## Priority buyers

[ASSUMPTION] Financial services: VP Customer Service or COO, championed by contact center and operations directors. Healthcare: VP Patient Access or COO, championed by scheduling and patient experience leaders. IT, security, privacy, and compliance teams influence both buying groups.

## Competitors that matter most

Competitor A, Competitor B, Competitor C, and the incumbent contact center or chatbot platform. Include building internally and maintaining current processes. [VERIFY] Research current overlap; do not assume feature superiority.

## The story we believe will win

[ASSUMPTION] “From customer request to completed task—across email, SMS, and web chat.” Lead with an administrative task customers or patients can finish, supported by clear action boundaries and contextual human handoffs. Financial services hero: collect and confirm receipt of loan documents. Healthcare hero: reschedule an appointment. Test whether these are compelling entry use cases and create distinct industry narratives.

## Claims this launch must not make

No invented customers, testimonials, ROI, or performance results. No guaranteed compliance, zero-error AI, or unverified certifications and integrations. No claim that the agent approves loans, provides investment advice, diagnoses conditions, recommends treatment, or replaces all staff. Fictional demos must remain labeled as illustrations.
