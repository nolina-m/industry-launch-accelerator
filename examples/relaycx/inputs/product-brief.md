## Problem (required)

### Problem statement

Customers receive answers but still struggle to complete service requests that span conversations, departments, and systems. Service teams manually retrieve records, collect missing information, and coordinate follow-ups, while customers repeat themselves across channels. [ASSUMPTION]

### Who has the problem

Customer service and operations teams at US regional banks, credit unions, and consumer lenders; patient access, scheduling, and billing teams at US outpatient provider groups and multisite specialty practices. Their customers and patients need routine administrative requests completed. [ASSUMPTION]

### Current workarounds

Shared inboxes, scripted chatbots, separate SMS tools, call centers, customer portals, and manual updates across CRM and operational systems. [ASSUMPTION]

## Solution (required)

### What it is

RelayCX is an agentic AI customer experience platform for email, SMS, and web chat. Its agents understand requests, retrieve approved information, and complete permitted actions in connected systems. Organizations define authentication requirements, action permissions, approval rules, and escalation paths. [ASSUMPTION: fictional product specification]

### Top capabilities

- Shared conversation context across email, SMS, and web chat, with identity verification before linking sensitive records.
- Multistep workflow execution, including appointment rescheduling and document collection.
- Answers grounded in approved knowledge and authorized operational records.
- Configurable action permissions, human approvals, handoffs, and audit logs.
- Reporting on verified completion, failed actions, repeat contacts, and service quality.

[ASSUMPTION: fictional capabilities, not demonstrated functionality]

### How it works

1. Receive a request and identify intent.
2. Apply the required identity and authorization checks.
3. Retrieve approved instructions and relevant records.
4. Collect missing information and execute permitted actions.
5. Verify the result, confirm completion, or transfer to staff with context.

## Anything else we should know

Financial services demo [ASSUMPTION]: A borrower emails about outstanding loan documents. After verification, RelayCX retrieves the checklist and sends a secure upload link. The borrower later asks by SMS whether the documents arrived. The agent checks receipt, updates the service case, and confirms that staff review is pending. Receipt never implies document approval or loan approval.

Healthcare demo [ASSUMPTION]: A patient starts on web chat to reschedule a specialist appointment. After verification, RelayCX checks permitted appointment types and available slots, obtains confirmation, books the change, and sends a minimal SMS confirmation with a secure link to approved preparation instructions. Clinical questions and scheduling exceptions transfer to staff.
