# Notes: financial-services

This pack is **scaffolding, not findings** (FR-IND-03). It tells the agent where to look: regulators, sources, data connectors, search seeds and vocabulary. Every trend and claim in a launch still comes from fresh, cited research.

- Regulations listed here are starting points. They're **not legal advice** and not a complete list. Check what applies to your product and regions.
- Connector series IDs were checked on 2026-10-18. Data series get renamed or discontinued over time; if one returns nothing, search the source for its replacement.
- To improve this pack, edit `pack.yaml` and run `PY scripts/validate.py industries/financial-services/pack.yaml`.
